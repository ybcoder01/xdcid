# XDCID Design System

> The current production language below remains the implementation baseline.
> A new product-wide identity exploration is available locally at
> `/design-lab`. It does not replace production routes until a direction is
> selected and approved.

## Product-wide identity exploration

The exploration applies one product truth across every screen: an XDCID is one
owner-bound identity with five independently selected, inspectable destination
records. The homepage, forms, dashboards and transaction states must make that
relationship visible instead of using connection lines as decoration.

### Direction 01 — Route Loom

**Idea:** Ownership woven into five precise routes.

- **Signature device:** the routeprint, an angular pattern generated by one
  identity and its five destination records.
- **Visual language:** deep carbon surfaces, acid chartreuse confirmation,
  exposed structural rules and dense functional typography.
- **Data language:** numbered routes, change previews and explicit proof strips.
- **Motion:** each route draws only after its record is confirmed; an edited
  route separates from the stable lines until its transaction settles.
- **Risk:** can become overly technical if human language and breathing room
  are removed.

### Direction 02 — Material Passport

**Idea:** A living identity record with visible provenance.

- **Signature device:** the identity record, a tangible owner-stamped document
  whose fields reveal ownership, routes and expiry.
- **Visual language:** warm paper, mineral green, registration red, editorial
  serif headlines and precise sans-serif controls.
- **Data language:** records, stamps, receipts and chronological registers.
- **Motion:** new evidence is stamped into the record; edits appear as visible
  amendments rather than silently replacing state.
- **Risk:** the metaphor must remain digitally credible and avoid nostalgia.

### Direction 03 — Proof Spectrum

**Idea:** One identity expressed through five verified signals.

- **Signature device:** a proof spectrograph. One owner-signed identity enters a
  verification slit and separates into five inspectable destination bands.
- **Visual language:** near-black field, restrained spectral accents and sharp
  modular bands instead of glowing crypto orbits.
- **Data language:** signal health, explicit route channels and visible state
  separation.
- **Motion:** pending changes flicker only within their channel; confirmed
  signals stabilize into a crisp band.
- **Risk:** spectral color must remain semantic and never become decorative AI
  gradient wallpaper.

## Shared experience architecture

- **Find:** search, availability and registration.
- **Manage:** dashboard, identity record, Primary ID, destinations, profiles,
  renewal, transfer and subdomains.
- **Pay:** send, signed payment links, receipts and payment history.
- **Build:** resolution, ownership and payment integration resources.

The local design lab provides Home, Dashboard, Identity, Send, Pay Links,
Subdomains, History and Developer views for every direction. These are design
prototypes only; wallet and transaction controls are intentionally inert.

## Product idea

XDCID is the shortest human-readable route to a wallet. The interface should make three ideas immediately clear: the identity is owned by the wallet, one `.xdc` name can resolve across five supported networks, and the owner chooses every destination.

The visual language is a network canvas rather than a conventional marketing page. Names and networks occupy a shared field around one clear action: finding and registering an XDCID.

## Experience principles

1. **One action first.** Search and registration remain the visual and interaction center.
2. **Show the network.** XDC, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, and Polygon are always represented with their real product logos.
3. **Depth without noise.** Floating `.xdc` names and orbital lines suggest an active identity network but stay visually quieter than the search.
4. **Wallet ownership is explicit.** Use precise phrases such as “Wallet-owned” and “Non-custodial”; avoid vague routing metaphors that imply XDCID controls user funds.
5. **Operational clarity over decoration.** Availability, price, term, and registration state must be legible before ornamental elements.

## Theme

Dark mode is the default. Light mode is a complete, switchable expression of the same design—not a separate layout.

| Token | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | `#071c1b` | `#f5f1e8` |
| Primary ink | `#f8fbfa` | `#151719` |
| Accent mint | `#70d8ca` | `#0d7775` |
| Action coral | `#ff735d` | `#ff735d` |
| Primary surface | `#0b2421` | white at 78% |
| Raised surface | `#102e2a` | white |
| Recessed surface | `#173934` | slate 50 |
| Structural line | mint at 13% | teal at 13% |

The theme control belongs in global navigation and must be reachable from every product route. Navigation, page canvas, surfaces, typography, borders, forms, and status panels must change together. Never switch only the page background while leaving light surfaces and text unchanged. Dark panels may appear inside the light theme only when they communicate a bounded functional state, not as an unrelated page theme.

## Typography

- Use the primary sans-serif for headlines, body copy, controls, and network names.
- Use monospace for compact labels, status messages, and technical metadata.
- Keep the homepage headline on one line: **“The shortest route to your wallet”**.
- Scale the headline fluidly. It should be prominent but leave visible space for the network and pricing state.
- Avoid oversized display text that forces the product action below the fold.

## Homepage composition

- The homepage fits within the viewport beneath navigation; it is not a long marketing page.
- Concentric rings establish the identity-routing field.
- The name selector is the origin point. A simple connector branches from it to one balanced row containing all five supported networks.
- Network nodes are never split into arbitrary left and right groups.
- Floating names are large, colorful identity cards placed around the perimeter with deliberate visual balance. They use slow continuous motion, remain outside the central reading path, disappear when screen space is constrained, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- The central order is: promise, supporting sentence, name search, availability, price/term, network logos, trust statement. Do not add a redundant eyebrow label above the promise.

## Search and registration states

The search responds automatically and uses these states:

- Empty: prompt the user to enter a name.
- Checking: communicate that registry status is being read.
- Available: show a direct registration action and reveal the live registration price.
- Registered: link to the existing profile.
- Reserved or review required: explain the registry condition without presenting a registration action.
- Error or unconfigured: state the problem without exposing raw provider errors.

When a name is available, reveal a compact quote panel with the live on-chain USD price and supported terms: 1, 3, 5, and 10 years. The selected term must carry into the registration page.

## Network identity

Use the existing production network-logo component for:

- XDC Network
- Ethereum
- Base
- Arbitrum
- Polygon

Network brand colors are accents, never full-page themes. Logos should remain visible on a neutral white badge in both modes.

## Responsive behavior

- Preserve the one-line headline by scaling it fluidly rather than wrapping it.
- Keep the connected five-network row below the search at every breakpoint; reduce logo and label size rather than changing its meaning.
- Hide decorative floating names on small screens.
- Price and term controls may stack, but the registration action must remain obvious.
- Maintain a minimum 44px target size for interactive controls.

## Accessibility

- The theme toggle announces the mode it will activate.
- Decorative rings and floating names are hidden from assistive technology.
- Availability changes use a polite live region.
- Do not encode availability or errors by color alone.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus and sufficient contrast in both modes.

## Consistency guardrails

- Reuse theme tokens instead of introducing page-specific background colors.
- In dark mode, distinguish page canvas, primary cards, raised cards, recessed panels, and form fields. Never collapse every light utility color into one dark green.
- Reuse production logos and registration logic; do not create decorative substitutes.
- Keep the primary search behavior identical between breakpoints and themes.
- Treat the homepage as the visual reference for future public-facing pages.
